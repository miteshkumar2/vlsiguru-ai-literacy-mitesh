# VLSIGuru AI Literacy – Week 01

## About This Project

This repository contains my work for the **VLSIGuru AI Literacy Week 01 Student Assessment**.

The purpose of this assignment is to understand the basics of Artificial Intelligence (AI), how AI works, its applications, and how to use it safely and responsibly in engineering.

I have completed nine missions covering AI fundamentals, Large Language Models (LLMs), AI applications, computing hardware, and responsible AI usage.

## Key Learnings

Through these missions, I learned:

* The basic meaning of Artificial Intelligence and its applications.
* The differences between AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI.
* How traditional programming differs from machine learning.
* How LLMs generate responses based on the context they receive.
* Why AI-generated answers should be verified using reliable sources.
* How AI applications, RAG, tools, and AI agents work at a basic level.
* The roles of CPUs, GPUs, NPUs, and memory in AI computing.
* How AI can assist with timing analysis in VLSI Physical Design.
* The importance of protecting confidential information and using AI responsibly.

## Application to My Engineering Career

As an Electronics and Communication Engineering graduate interested in VLSI Physical Design, I want to understand how AI can support engineering tasks.

AI may help engineers analyze timing reports, identify possible issues, and organize technical information. However, engineers must verify the results and ensure that any design changes are technically correct.

## Conclusion

This assignment helped me build a basic understanding of Artificial Intelligence and its role in engineering. I learned that AI is a useful assistant, but its answers are not always correct. Therefore, I should verify important information, protect confidential data, and understand the work I submit.

I will continue learning how to use AI effectively, safely, and responsibly in my engineering career.
