# Mission 5: Can AI Be Confidently Wrong?

## 1. Prompt

**Question asked to the AI assistant:**

Do humans have five senses?

## 2. AI Answer

Yes, humans have five main senses: sight, hearing, smell, taste, and touch. These senses help us understand and interact with the world around us.

## 3. Verification Source

**Source:** [Britannica — Human Sensory Reception](https://www.britannica.com/science/human-sensory-reception)

Britannica explains how humans receive information through different sensory systems. Human sensory abilities include sight, hearing, smell, taste, touch, and other forms of sensation.

## 4. Verification Result

**Result: Incomplete.**

The AI's answer is correct in describing the five traditional senses. However, humans also have other sensory abilities, such as detecting body position and balance. Therefore, saying humans have only five senses is an oversimplification.

## 5. Lesson Learned

I learned that an AI answer can sound correct but still leave out important information. Even when an answer is familiar, checking a reliable source helps me understand the topic more accurately.
