# Mission 9: Build Your Own AI-Use Rule

## My 5-Rule AI Working Agreement

1. **Understand Before Using:** I will use AI to understand engineering concepts in simple words, but I will make sure I understand the answer myself.

2. **Verify Important Information:** I will check important technical answers using reliable sources before trusting them. In Mission 5, I learned that AI can give incomplete answers, as not all birds can fly.

3. **Protect Confidential Information:** I will not share confidential company data, private information, passwords, or unpublished engineering designs with AI tools.

4. **Take Responsibility for My Work:** I will review AI-generated answers, correct mistakes, and take responsibility for the final assignment or engineering work I submit.

5. **Use AI as a Learning Assistant:** I will use AI to learn, solve problems, and improve my skills rather than blindly copying its answers.

## Conclusion

These five rules will help me use AI safely and responsibly during my engineering learning. I will treat AI as a helpful assistant, verify its important claims, protect confidential information, and make sure I understand and take responsibility for my final work.
