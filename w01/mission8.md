# Mission 8: Where Could AI Help in My VLSI Track?

## Chosen Area: Physical Design (PD)

| Item                              | Explanation                                                                                                                                  |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Task                              | Identifying timing violations in a chip design.                                                                                              |
| How AI might help                 | AI can analyse timing reports and help identify paths that may cause timing violations, making debugging faster.                             |
| Why human knowledge still matters | A VLSI engineer must understand timing constraints and chip design to verify AI suggestions and decide which changes are safe and effective. |

## Conclusion

AI can help physical design engineers analyse reports and identify possible timing problems more quickly, but human knowledge is still necessary to verify the results and make correct design decisions.
